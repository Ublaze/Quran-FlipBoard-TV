Quran FlipBoard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Overview

Quran FlipBoard is a free ambient display application for LG webOS TVs that displays verses from the Holy Quran. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Data Collection

Quran FlipBoard does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No personal information is collected
  • No usage analytics or telemetry is gathered
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No user accounts or registration is required
  • No network requests are made — the app is fully offline
  • No personal data is stored. The app only saves its own display preferences (show mode, pace, order, sound) and the position of the verse on screen, locally on the TV, so it can continue where it left off. This never leaves the TV and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, advertising networks, or analytics platforms.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The content (Quran verses) is suitable for all ages.

Content

The app displays verses from the Holy Quran with Arabic text and the Sahih International English translation. All content is bundled within the application; nothing is downloaded at runtime.

Open Source Fonts

The app uses the Amiri and Amiri Quran fonts, licensed under the SIL Open Font License (OFL). No data is transmitted in relation to font usage.

Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Since the app collects no data, significant changes are unlikely.
