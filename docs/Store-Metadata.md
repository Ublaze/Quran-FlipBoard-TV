## Full Description
Transform your LG TV into a beautiful ambient Quran display. Quran FlipBoard presents verses from the Holy Quran using a stunning retro split-flap (airport departure board) animation.

Features:
- 113 curated verses from 74 surahs with Arabic calligraphy and English translation
- Authentic split-flap mechanical animation with satisfying sound
- Elegant Amiri Quran font for proper Arabic diacritical marks
- Multiple display modes: Arabic + English, Arabic only, or English only
- Adjustable speed: Fast, Normal, Slow, or Contemplative
- OLED-friendly dark theme with gold accents
- Fully offline — no internet required
- Simple D-pad and Magic Remote navigation

Perfect as a screensaver, ambient display during gatherings, or for daily Quran reading reminders.
